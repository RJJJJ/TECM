# Human-in-the-loop Policy

## Policy

- AI only drafts internal suggestions.
- Staff reviews every suggestion before parent contact.
- Staff manually contacts parent through WeChat / WhatsApp / phone.
- No automatic WeChat message is sent by TECM v1.1 automation.
- Parent-facing notifications only use formal booking confirmation content.
- Internal AI notes must never be shown to parents.
- Production scheduler requires safety review before activation.

## Operational rules

- Keep suggested messages concise and staff-copyable.
- Avoid claims about guaranteed seats or unverified schedules.
- Record task status after staff action.
- Dismiss irrelevant or stale suggestions.
